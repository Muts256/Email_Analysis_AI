#### Email_Analysis_AI

Phishing remains the number one way attackers get into organisations, and the volume of emails alone is enough to overwhelm any SOC team. Triage is repetitive, draining, and surprisingly easy to get wrong when you're on your tenth suspicious email of the day. Can AI help fix that? This project puts that question to the test, documenting a practical workflow and giving an honest verdict on whether it actually makes analysts faster, sharper, and less burnt out
